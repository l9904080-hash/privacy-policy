Privacy Policy

Photo / Signature Extractor

This Privacy Policy explains how the Photo / Signature Extractor app ("the App") handles your information.

1. No data leaves your device

All processing — photo/signature detection, cropping, resizing, compression, and PDF/ZIP creation — happens entirely on your device. The App does not upload your images, documents, or any personal data to any server, and does not have a backend.

2. Camera permission

The App may request camera access so you can photograph a physical sheet of photos/signatures directly within the App. Captured images are used only for the current session and are not stored anywhere except on your own device, and only for as long as you keep them there.

3. Storage permission

The App may request storage access to let you select image files to process and to save the exported ZIP/PDF files it creates. Files you export are saved directly to your device's storage under your control.

4. No third-party sharing

The App does not share, sell, or transmit any of your data to third parties, advertisers, or analytics services, because no data is ever collected in the first place.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it performs no data collection at all.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this same URL with an updated date below.

7. Contact
